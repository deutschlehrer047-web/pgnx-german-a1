About PGNX

Pak-German Nexus · Student Community & Support

PGNX – Pak-German Nexus is a student community that supports students on their journey to Germany.

The community provides guidance, information and peer support related to studying in Germany, universities, visa processes, waiting-list updates and life in Germany. PGNX also has groups for different German cities, where students from Pakistan and Germany can connect, share experiences and help each other.

As part of the community, we have also started a free German language learning initiative. Students can learn German step by step through structured lessons, practice activities and useful learning materials.
